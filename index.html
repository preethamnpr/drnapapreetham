About Dr. Napa Preetham

Comprehensive Pulmonary Medicine with Compassionate, Evidence-Based Intensive Care

Dr. Napa Preetham is a Consultant Pulmonologist and Critical Care Specialist practicing at Aster Narayanadri Hospital, Tirupati. He blends clinical precision with cutting-edge, minimally invasive thoracic interventions to diagnose and manage both complex chronic respiratory ailments and life-threatening critical illnesses.

After graduating with his MBBS from the prestigious S.V. Medical College (Tirupati), Dr. Preetham pursued his post-graduate residency in Pulmonary Medicine (DNB) at Aster Aadhar Hospital, Kolhapur. He underwent intensive training under veteran interventionalists in rigid bronchoscopy, mediastinal lymph node staging via EBUS, medical thoracoscopy, and airway tumor debulking. He subsequently earned the prestigious IDCCM (Indian Diploma in Critical Care Medicine), honing expertise in severe ARDS, invasive mechanical ventilation, and multi-organ life support.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is EBUS-TBNA and is it painful?

Endobronchial Ultrasound (EBUS) allows examination of the lungs and lymph nodes from inside the airways using an ultrasound-equipped bronchoscope. It is performed under conscious sedation and local anesthesia, making it virtually pain-free without external chest cuts. Most patients return home the same day.

When should I consult a pulmonologist instead of a general physician?

You should see a pulmonologist if you have a cough lasting over 3 weeks, unexplained shortness of breath during routine activity, recurrent chest infections, wheezing, coughing up blood, abnormal chest X-ray/CT shadows, or persistent heavy snoring accompanied by daytime sleepiness.

How do I prepare for a consultation at OPD Room 7?

Please carry all previous chest X-rays, CT films, Spirometry/PFT reports, blood test results, and your current list of medications or inhalers. Fasting is usually not required unless you are scheduled for a same-day bronchoscopy or CT contrast scan.
