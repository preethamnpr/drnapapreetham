About Dr. Napa Preetham

Comprehensive Pulmonary Medicine with Compassionate, Evidence-Based Intensive Care

Dr. Napa Preetham is a Consultant Clinical & Interventional Pulmonologist and Critical Care Specialist practicing at Aster Narayanadri Hospital, Tirupati. He blends clinical precision with cutting-edge, minimally invasive thoracic interventions to diagnose and manage both complex chronic respiratory ailments and life-threatening critical illnesses.

After graduating with his MBBS from the prestigious S.V. Medical College (Tirupati), Dr. Preetham pursued his post-graduate residency in Pulmonary Medicine (DNB) at Aster Aadhar Hospital, Kolhapur. He underwent intensive training under veteran interventionalists in rigid bronchoscopy, mediastinal lymph node staging via EBUS, medical thoracoscopy, and airway tumor debulking. He subsequently earned the prestigious IDCCM (Indian Diploma in Critical Care Medicine), honing expertise in severe ARDS, invasive mechanical ventilation, and multi-organ life support.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about OPD consultations, sleep studies, bronchoscopy, and diagnostic preparations.

What should I bring for my first Pulmonology OPD consultation?

Please bring any previous Chest X-rays, HRCT Thorax scans (including films or CDs), Pulmonary Function Test (Spirometry) reports, blood investigations, and all inhalers or medications you currently take. Visit OPD Room 7 at Aster Narayanadri Hospital, Tirupati.

How is a Sleep Study (Polysomnography) performed under Complete Sleep Diagnostic Care?

Polysomnography is a completely painless, non-invasive overnight test conducted in a comfortable private sleep suite. Small surface sensors monitor your brain waves, breathing airflow, oxygen saturation, heart rate, and snoring while you sleep naturally, allowing accurate diagnosis of Obstructive Sleep Apnea (OSA) and customized CPAP/BiPAP pressure settings.

Are Bronchoscopy and EBUS-TBNA painful or do they require major surgery?

No. Flexible Bronchoscopy and Endobronchial Ultrasound (EBUS-TBNA) are minimally invasive, scarless keyhole endoscopic procedures performed under local airway anesthesia and conscious sedation. Most patients experience minimal discomfort and return home the very same day after a brief recovery observation.

When should I consult a pulmonologist for a persistent cough?

Any cough lasting longer than 3 weeks warrants a specialist evaluation, and a cough exceeding 8 weeks is classified as chronic. Seek immediate medical care if your cough is accompanied by blood in sputum, progressive breathlessness, chest pain, unexplained weight loss, or persistent evening fever.

Are inhalers safe and habit-forming for Asthma or COPD?

Inhalers are the safest and most effective gold-standard treatment for Asthma and COPD—they are not addictive or habit-forming. Because inhaled medicine delivers microgram doses directly to the airways, it controls lung inflammation with virtually none of the systemic side effects of oral tablets or syrups.
